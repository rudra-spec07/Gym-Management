# 08. Open Business Decisions (OBD Log)

This document serves as the formal log of unresolved product rules, business logic ambiguities, and open stakeholder decisions. Every item flagged here must be reviewed and confirmed by business stakeholders prior to technical implementation.

---

## 📋 Open Business Decision Log

| OBD ID | Module | Title / Description | Proposed Options | Impact if Unresolved |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `OBD-STAR-001` | **Star / Score System** | **Star Score Floor & Negative Values**: Can a member's Star score become negative if they miss multiple days? | **Option A**: Allow negative scores (e.g. -10 Stars).<br>**Option B**: Cap minimum score at 0 Stars (Floor = 0). *(Recommended)* | Affects UI score rendering and gamification motivation. |
| `OBD-STAR-002` | **Star / Score System** | **Streak vs. Star Score Relationship**: Is "Streak" purely consecutive attendance days, or does missing a day reset the Star Score? | **Option A**: Streak resets to 0 on a missed day, but Star Score accumulates continuously (+1 / -2). *(Recommended)*<br>**Option B**: Streak and Star Score are completely unified. | Affects database model attributes and UI display cards. |
| `OBD-STREAK-001` | **Streak Engine** | **Missed Day Streak Reset**: Does 1 missed eligible gym day completely reset the attendance streak to 0? | **Option A**: Yes, 1 missed eligible day resets streak to 0.<br>**Option B**: Member can use a "Streak Freeze / Shield" (1 freeze per month). | Core gamification engine algorithm design. |
| `OBD-LEAVE-001` | **Attendance / Inactivity** | **Approved Leaves vs. Unapproved Absences**: Are approved member leaves treated differently from unapproved absences? | **Option A**: Admin can log "Approved Leave", preserving streak and pausing 10-day inactivity counter.<br>**Option B**: All missed days count equally toward streak reset and inactivity. | Impacts admin portal workflow and inactivity calculation engine. |
| `OBD-HOLIDAY-001` | **Attendance Engine** | **Special Gym Closures**: What happens when the gym is closed for a special holiday (e.g., Diwali, Christmas)? | **Option A**: Admin marks "Special Closure" in calendar; treated identically to Sunday (zero impact).<br>**Option B**: Requires manual streak restoration by admin. | Determines admin calendar feature scope. |
| `OBD-ATT-001` | **Attendance Engine** | **Manual Attendance Correction Policy**: Can admin manually correct past attendance logs? | **Option A**: Admin can manually edit any past date with required audit log.<br>**Option B**: Admin can only correct attendance within 48 hours of occurrence. | Security and audit trail requirements. |
| `OBD-JOIN-001` | **Streak Engine** | **Mid-Week Onboarding Streak Calculation**: How are streaks and stars calculated for a member who joins midway through a week (e.g., Wednesday)? | **Option A**: Streak starts from 1 on first check-in date; prior days in week ignored.<br>**Option B**: Past days in week marked as unexcused. | Initial user onboarding experience and fair score calculation. |
| `OBD-EXP-001` | **Membership Lifecycle** | **Membership Expiry Streak Behavior**: What happens to a member's streak and star score when their membership expires? | **Option A**: Streak and Stars frozen until membership renewed.<br>**Option B**: Streak resets to 0 after 7 days of non-renewal. | Retention and renewal incentive mechanics. |
| `OBD-PROD-001` | **Product Store** | **Product Fulfillment Scope**: Should member product purchase be online payment + home delivery, OR online request + front-desk pickup & payment? | **Option A**: Front-desk pickup & cash/UPI payment on delivery.<br>**Option B**: Full online payment via Razorpay + inventory fulfillment. | Scope of product management and payment gateway routing. |
| `OBD-NFR-001` | **Architecture** | **Multi-Gym / Multi-Tenant Support**: Is this application strictly single-gym (1 location) for MVP, or multi-tenant (multiple gym branches)? | **Option A**: Single-Gym instance for MVP.<br>**Option B**: Multi-tenant schema with `GymId` foreign key across all tables. | Database schema design and Prisma model multi-tenancy architecture. |
